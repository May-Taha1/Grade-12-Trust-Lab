Trust Lab · Grade 12 AI

Four stations: audit the risks, measure fairness, run and verify the matcher, architect and pressure-test. Made-up data only. Nothing is sent anywhere: no login, no tracking.

H1 · Risk Audit

A company uses an AI screener to shortlist CVs. First find where each problem enters the pipeline, then rate and rank the risks like an auditor.

H2 · Fairness Lab

Build the confusion matrix for two groups, compute the gap in percentage points, then fix the stage where bias entered without breaking something else.

H3 · Matcher Run

A surplus-food matcher supports Ne'ma: it pairs donated items with charities. Inspect the data, run it end to end with checks at each hand-off, compute precision, then handle a new allergen constraint.

H4 · Architect & Pressure-Test

Plan a signature solution, prototype it with a test log, break it on purpose with hard cases, and add a fallback path.

Illustrative scenarios. No real people, names or personal data. Record your numbers on your worksheet. Trust Lab · Grade 12 · Main course · Weeks 5–7. Works offline once loaded.
